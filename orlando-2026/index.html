⛈️ Rain Plan — the June Special

June in Orlando means a thunderstorm most afternoons, usually rolling through ~2–4 PM and gone within the hour. Don't bail — duck into something indoors, let it pass, and enjoy the thinner crowds after. Outdoor coasters pause for lightning; indoor rides keep right on running.

🧥 Ponchos: pack them from home — they're ~$15 apiece in the parks.

Day 2 · Universal × 2
Sunday, June 7, 2026
Universal Studios & Islands of Adventure, one big day
🎟️ 1-day park-to-park ticket
✨ Highlights & things to consider
Must-do
🏍️ Hagrid's Magical Creatures Motorbike Adventure (IOA)
The crowd-pleaser of the whole resort — fast but not scary-fast. 48" height req, so the whole crew clears it. Hit it early or brace for the longest line on property.
Big thrill
🦖 VelociCoaster (IOA)
70 mph, a 155-ft top hat, and a barrel roll over the lagoon — for the daredevil wing of the group. Spectators: the bridge by the paddock is prime viewing.
Everyone
🐉 Escape from Gringotts (USF)
Coaster-meets-dark-ride underneath Diagon Alley. Above ground, the dragon breathes real fire about every 10 minutes — listen for the rumble.
Don't skip
🚂 Hogwarts Express — ride it BOTH directions
King's Cross ↔ Hogsmeade is a different show each way, and it's the easiest way to hop parks. Needs the park-to-park ticket — we've got it.
Classics
🕷️ Spider-Man (IOA) + 🧟 Revenge of the Mummy (USF)
Two all-timer indoor rides — and two excellent blasts of air conditioning.
Wet!
💦 Jurassic Park River Adventure & Popeye's barges (IOA)
"Wet" undersells it — you will be drenched. Save for the hottest stretch of the afternoon.
All ages
🍌 Minion Land + Men in Black: Alien Attack (USF)
MIB is competitive laser-blasting with a score — family leaderboard rules apply.
Snack
🍺 Butterbeer + Florean Fortescue's ice cream (Diagon Alley)
Frozen butterbeer > regular butterbeer. This is the hill we die on.

Day 4 · Hollywood Studios + EPCOT
Tuesday, June 9, 2026
Two parks, one epic hop
🎟️ Disney Park Hopper · day 1 of 3
✨ Highlights & things to consider
Must-do
🚀 Star Wars: Rise of the Resistance (Hollywood Studios)
Disney's most ambitious ride — part walkthrough, part dark ride, all spectacle. Rope-drop it or grab Lightning Lane; it's everyone's first pick.
Everyone
🐶 Slinky Dog Dash
The family coaster that makes the whole group grin — gentle enough for the cautious, fun enough for the rest.
Big thrill
🗼 The Twilight Zone Tower of Terror
The drop sequence is randomized — no two rides are the same. Stomachs optional, screams guaranteed.
Crew up
🛸 Millennium Falcon: Smugglers Run + 🚂 Mickey & Minnie's Runaway Railway
On the Falcon everyone gets an actual job — pilots steer, engineers push the blinky buttons. Runaway Railway is trackless, clever, and good for all ages.
The hop
🚡 Take the Skyliner to EPCOT
The gondola from HS to EPCOT's back door is an attraction in itself — aim for early afternoon.
Heads-up
🌌 Guardians of the Galaxy: Cosmic Rewind
Likely needs a virtual queue — check the app at 7 AM and again at 1 PM sharp. The coaster cars rotate mid-ride; intense but smooth, and the soundtrack slaps.
EPCOT trio
🏎️ Test Track · ❄️ Frozen Ever After · 🐀 Remy's Ratatouille Adventure
Design-your-own car races, a singalong boat, and a shrunk-to-rat-size scamper through a Paris kitchen — something for every faction.
Evening
🍜 Graze World Showcase, then Luminous fireworks on the lagoon
11 countries, every snack counts. Stake out a lagoon spot ~30 min before show time.
Quick links
🚗 Directions 🕐 Park hours ⏱️ HS waits ⏱️ EPCOT waits 🍔 Mobile order 🎮 Queue games
🗼
The Tower of Terror is exactly 199 feet tall — one foot shorter than the height that would legally require a blinking aircraft-warning light on top (which would ruin the spooky 1939 vibe).
🛸
The Millennium Falcon in Galaxy's Edge is full size — over 100 feet across — the first complete, full-scale Falcon ever built.
⚪
Spaceship Earth (the big golf ball) is wrapped in 11,324 aluminum triangles, with hidden gutters that swallow rainwater so it never pours off the sides — it drains into World Showcase Lagoon.
🏙️
EPCOT stands for Experimental Prototype Community of Tomorrow — Walt Disney originally planned it as a real, functioning city of 20,000 people.
🌎
World Showcase has 11 country pavilions, and many cast members working each pavilion are from that country, on cultural exchange programs.
Day 5 · Animal Kingdom
Wednesday, June 10, 2026
Safaris, Pandora, and a yeti
🎟️ Disney Park Hopper · day 2 of 3
✨ Highlights & things to consider
Go early
🦒 Kilimanjaro Safaris
A real 110-acre savanna — giraffes, lions, elephants, rhinos. Animals are most active in the cool of the morning, so make this the first stop.
Must-do
🪂 Avatar Flight of Passage (Pandora)
Arguably Disney's best ride, period — you're on the back of a banshee. Worth the wait or the Lightning Lane. 44" height req.
All ages
🛶 Na'vi River Journey (Pandora)
A gentle float through a glowing bioluminescent forest — the looker of the park, zero thrills required.
Thrill
🏔️ Expedition Everest
Forwards, then BACKWARDS in the dark, then a yeti. 44" — the 8-year-olds who like coasters will be fine; so will most mid-40s knees.
Wet!
🌊 Kali River Rapids
You WILL get soaked — ponchos or sacrificial clothes. June in Florida = you'll dry… eventually.
Show
🦁 Festival of the Lion King
Broadway-caliber singers and acrobats, 30 minutes of A/C — the perfect afternoon-storm shelter.
Wander
🦍 Gorilla Falls & Maharajah Jungle Trek walking trails
Gorillas, tigers, komodo dragons, giant bats — self-paced walkthroughs most people speed past. Don't.
Kids (sure)
🥾 Wilderness Explorers badge hunt
A park-wide scavenger hunt with a sticker book — nominally for the kids, historically taken over by the adults. AK closes earlier than other parks, so it's pool + grill night after.
Quick links
🚗 Directions 🕐 Park hours ⏱️ AK waits 🍔 Mobile order 🎮 Queue games
📐
Animal Kingdom is the largest Disney theme park on Earth — about 580 acres. Magic Kingdom would fit inside it five times.
🌳
The Tree of Life is 145 feet tall with over 300 animals carved into its trunk and branches — and its core is engineered like an offshore oil platform so it can hold a theater inside.
🦨
The park opened on Earth Day — April 22, 1998 — and is home to over 2,000 real animals representing 300+ species.
🏔️
Expedition Everest is reported to be the most expensive roller coaster ever built (~$100M). Its yeti was the largest, most complex animatronic Disney ever made — until it broke and became the beloved strobe-lit "Disco Yeti."
🦋
There are no balloons or plastic straws anywhere in the park — they could harm the animals.

Day 7 · Epic Universe
Friday, June 12, 2026
Orlando's newest park — portals to five worlds
🎟️ 1-day single-park ticket
✨ Highlights & things to consider
First!
⏰ Arrive before gates open, then beeline to 🍄 Super Nintendo World
This park is the hot ticket and the Mushroom Kingdom fills first — get there early or expect a long wait just to walk in.
Everyone
🏎️ Mario Kart: Bowser's Challenge + Power-Up Bands
AR headset kart battle the whole crew can ride. The Power-Up Bands track your score across the land — the family leaderboard is very much a competition.
Sleeper hit
🦍 Mine-Cart Madness (Donkey Kong Country)
The cart "jumps" gaps in the track — looks impossible, feels hilarious, fine for everyone.
Big thrill
🌠 Stardust Racers
Dual-launch racing coaster — two trains dogfight at 62 mph over 133-ft peaks. Ride it by day, then again after dark when it glows.
All ages
🐉 Isle of Berk — Hiccup's Wing Gliders + live dragons everywhere
The How to Train Your Dragon land is the warmest of the five worlds — shows, splash zones, and a family coaster that's better than it needs to be.
Don't skip
🧙 Ministry of Magic — Battle at the Ministry
1920s wizarding Paris; the queue through the Ministry is half the attraction. Expect a virtual line in the app.
Brave-ish
🧟 Dark Universe — Monsters Unchained: The Frankenstein Experiment
Intense animatronics and dark scenes in Darkmoor village — great for 8-year-olds who like spooky, skippable for ones who don't.
Stay late
🎠 Constellation Carousel, the fountains, and the nighttime show over the water
Celestial Park is the breather between portals — and the whole park is a different place after dark.
Quick links
🚗 Directions 🌌 Official page 📱 Universal app ⏱️ Wait times 🎮 Queue games
🆕
Epic Universe opened May 22, 2025 — the first brand-new major theme park in Orlando since Islands of Adventure in 1999. We're visiting in its second-ever summer.
🌀
Each of the four worlds is entered through a giant portal — the theming, music, and even pavement change the instant you cross through.
🏁
Stardust Racers is a dual-launch racing coaster: two trains "race" side by side at up to 62 mph over 133-ft peaks, swapping sides mid-ride in a celestial dogfight.
🏨
The Helios Grand Hotel is built into the back of the park with its own private park entrance — its rooftop bar overlooks the whole nighttime show.
⚡
The Power-Up Bands in Super Nintendo World keep score across the whole land — your family leaderboard is very much a competition.
Day 8 · Magic Kingdom
Saturday, June 13, 2026
The classic — plus circle back for anything we missed
🎟️ Disney Park Hopper · day 3 of 3
✨ Highlights & things to consider
Heads-up
🚴 TRON Lightcycle/Run — virtual queue drops at 7 AM
Phones out at breakfast. You straddle the bike like an actual lightcycle; the launch is the best 3 seconds at Magic Kingdom.
Must-do
⛏️ Seven Dwarfs Mine Train
The swinging mine carts are gentle enough for everyone and the line is eternal — Lightning Lane it or rope-drop it.
Classics
🚀 Space Mountain + 🚂 Big Thunder Mountain
Coasters in the dark and runaway trains — 1970s thrills that still absolutely hold up.
All-timers
🏴‍☠️ Pirates of the Caribbean + 👻 Haunted Mansion
All ages, no height requirement, endlessly rewatchable — count the hidden details. Grim grinning ghosts come out to socialize.
Pure silly
🛥️ Jungle Cruise
The puns are the point. The backside of water never gets old.
A/C break
🚝 PeopleMover + Carousel of Progress
The most underrated air-conditioned sitting at Disney World. There's a great big beautiful tomorrow, and the kids will be humming it for days.
Snack
🍍 Dole Whip in Adventureland
Non-negotiable. Last Disney day — the Park Hopper also lets us circle back to EPCOT / HS / AK to clear the wish list (monorail to EPCOT is the classic hop).
Finale
🎆 Happily Ever After fireworks over the castle
The grand finale of theme-park week. Hub grass or Main Street view — stake out a spot early. Tissues optional but likely.

🕳️
You never walk on the ground floor: the whole park sits on top of the utilidors, a tunnel network where cast members, costumes, and trash travel out of sight. Main Street is technically the second story.
🏰
Cinderella Castle contains zero bricks — it's fiberglass over a steel frame, 189 feet tall, and was built in about 18 months. Forced perspective makes it look much taller.
👑
Magic Kingdom is the most-visited theme park on the planet — roughly 17–18 million guests a year.
🗑️
Trash cans are placed about every 30 feet — supposedly the distance Walt observed a person would carry trash before dropping it.
😢
Walt Disney never saw it: he died in 1966, five years before the park opened on October 1, 1971. His brother Roy delayed retirement to finish it and insisted it be named Walt Disney World.
