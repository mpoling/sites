⛈️ Rain Plan — the June Special

June in Orlando means a thunderstorm most afternoons, usually rolling through ~2–4 PM and gone within the hour. Don't bail — duck into something indoors, let it pass, and enjoy the thinner crowds after. Outdoor coasters pause for lightning; indoor rides keep right on running.

🧥 Ponchos: pack them from home — they're ~$15 apiece in the parks.

📐
Animal Kingdom is the largest Disney theme park on Earth — about 580 acres. Magic Kingdom would fit inside it five times.
🌳
The Tree of Life is 145 feet tall with over 300 animals carved into its trunk and branches — and its core is engineered like an offshore oil platform so it can hold a theater inside.
🦨
The park opened on Earth Day — April 22, 1998 — and is home to over 2,000 real animals representing 300+ species.
🏔️
Expedition Everest is reported to be the most expensive roller coaster ever built (~$100M). Its yeti was the largest, most complex animatronic Disney ever made — until it broke and became the beloved strobe-lit "Disco Yeti."
🦋
There are no balloons or plastic straws anywhere in the park — they could harm the animals.

🆕
Epic Universe opened May 22, 2025 — the first brand-new major theme park in Orlando since Islands of Adventure in 1999. We're visiting in its second-ever summer.
🌀
Each of the four worlds is entered through a giant portal — the theming, music, and even pavement change the instant you cross through.
🏁
Stardust Racers is a dual-launch racing coaster: two trains "race" side by side at up to 62 mph over 133-ft peaks, swapping sides mid-ride in a celestial dogfight.
🏨
The Helios Grand Hotel is built into the back of the park with its own private park entrance — its rooftop bar overlooks the whole nighttime show.
⚡
The Power-Up Bands in Super Nintendo World keep score across the whole land — your family leaderboard is very much a competition.

🕳️
You never walk on the ground floor: the whole park sits on top of the utilidors, a tunnel network where cast members, costumes, and trash travel out of sight. Main Street is technically the second story.
🏰
Cinderella Castle contains zero bricks — it's fiberglass over a steel frame, 189 feet tall, and was built in about 18 months. Forced perspective makes it look much taller.
👑
Magic Kingdom is the most-visited theme park on the planet — roughly 17–18 million guests a year.
🗑️
Trash cans are placed about every 30 feet — supposedly the distance Walt observed a person would carry trash before dropping it.
😢
Walt Disney never saw it: he died in 1966, five years before the park opened on October 1, 1971. His brother Roy delayed retirement to finish it and insisted it be named Walt Disney World.

St. Petersburg holds the Guinness World Record for most consecutive days of sunshine: 768 days (1967–1969). Hence the nickname "The Sunshine City."
🗞️
A local paper, The Evening Independent, famously gave the paper away free on any day the sun didn't shine — it only had to pay up a handful of times a year.
